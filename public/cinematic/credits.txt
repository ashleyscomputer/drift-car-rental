Presentation note
The homepage uses these two visual states of the same vehicle to create a lightweight 2.5D cinematic sequence: approach, zoom, gullwing reveal and detail movement. CSS crops, tonal grading, masks, overlays and motion are non-destructive presentation treatments and do not imply endorsement by the photographers, Wikimedia Commons or Mercedes-Benz.

The SLS AMG is presented as a Drift Experience showcase and is not represented as currently bookable rental inventory.
