Presentation note
The site applies non-destructive CSS crops, tonal grading, masks, overlays and motion to the downloaded photographs. Those presentation treatments do not imply endorsement by the photographers, Wikimedia Commons, Mercedes-Benz or Ferrari. The Ferrari chapter is described as a Drift Experience with a carbon-inspired / Mansory-inspired campaign attitude only; it does not claim that the photographed car is a Mansory conversion.
